Campus Soccer Night

Date and Time: September 25, 2026 at 5:00 PM

Location: University Recreation Field

Campus Soccer Night is a recreational event where Gifted University students can meet other students, play soccer, and enjoy a fun evening on campus. No previous experience is required, and students of all skill levels are welcome to participate.

About the Event

Campus Soccer Night gives students an opportunity to take a break from their schoolwork and participate in a friendly soccer event. Students can play in organized games, practice their skills, or socialize with other members of the university community.

The event is designed to be welcoming to both experienced players and students who are trying soccer for the first time. Participants are encouraged to bring comfortable athletic clothing, water, and a positive attitude.

Attendee Expectations

  • Arrive at least 15 minutes before the event begins.
  • Wear comfortable clothing and athletic shoes(Optional).
  • Bring a water bottle and stay hydrated.
  • Respect other players, volunteers, and event staff.
  • Follow the instructions of event organizers.
  • Help keep the recreation field clean.

Event Schedule

  • 5:00 PM — Check-in and team assignments
  • 5:15 PM — Warm-up and event instructions
  • 5:30 PM — Recreational games begin
  • 6:30 PM — Short break and team rotation
  • 7:00 PM — Final games and closing activities

Accessibility and Participation

Gifted University is committed to making campus activities accessible to all students. Students who need accommodations should contact the event organizers before attending so that appropriate arrangements can be made.

Organizers can be contacted at RSoccer@gifteduniversity.edu.

Students do not need previous soccer experience to participate. Anyone who would rather watch or socialize is also welcome to attend the event.
