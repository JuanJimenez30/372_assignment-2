About Campus Event Guide

The Gifted University Event Home Page helps students discover activities, organizations, and opportunities happening throughout the university. The site is designed to make it easier for students to find events that match their interests.

Whether you are looking for something social, educational, recreational, or community-focused, the guide provides a convenient place to explore upcoming activities.

  • Opportunities to connect with other students
